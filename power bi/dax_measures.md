### 1. Active Customers

Measures the number of distinct customers who have made purchases during the selected reporting period.

**DAX Formula:**

```dax
Active Customers =
DISTINCTCOUNT(transactions[customer_id])
```

This KPI helps management understand how many unique customers are actively purchasing from the business. It can be used to monitor customer activity, evaluate customer engagement and identify changes in the customer base.

### 2. Items Count Sold

Calculates the total quantity of items sold across all recorded transactions.

**DAX Formula:**

```dax
Items count sold =
SUM(transaction_items[quantity])
```

This KPI helps management understand the total volume of products sold. It can be used to monitor sales volume, evaluate product demand and support inventory planning and stock management decisions.

### 3. Non-Active Customers

Calculates the number of customers who are not currently purchasing from the business by subtracting active customers from the total customer base.

**DAX Formula:**

```dax
Non-Active Customers =
[Total Customers] - [Active Customers]
```
This KPI helps management identify customers within the customer base who have not made purchases during the selected reporting period. It can help identify opportunities for customer re-engagement, retention campaigns and targeted marketing initiatives.

### 4. Profit (Gross Profit)

Calculates the gross profit generated by the business by subtracting the total cost of products sold from the revenue generated.

**DAX Formula:**

```dax
Profit =
[Revenue] - [Sales Cost]
```
This KPI Measures the amount remaining after deducting the direct cost of products sold from revenue. It helps management evaluate profitability across stores, product categories and reporting periods.

### 5. Profit Margin

Calculates the proportion of revenue retained as gross profit after deducting the cost of products sold.

**DAX Formula:**

```dax
Profit Margin =
DIVIDE([Profit], [Revenue])
```

This KPI Measures how much gross profit the business generates from each rand of revenue. It helps management assess profitability and compare performance across stores, product categories and reporting periods.

### 6. Revenue

Calculates the total sales revenue generated from products sold by multiplying the actual selling price per unit by the quantity sold.

**DAX Formula:**

```dax
Revenue =
SUMX(
    transaction_items,
    transaction_items[unit_price_at_sale] * transaction_items[quantity]
)
```

This KPI Measures the total monetary value of sales generated during the selected reporting period. It helps management evaluate sales performance across stores, product categories and time periods.

### 7. Total Discount Applied

Calculates the total monetary value of discounts applied to products across all transactions.

**DAX Formula:**

```dax
Total Discount Applied =
SUM(transaction_items[discount_applied])
```

This KPI Measures the total discounts offered to customers during the selected reporting period. It helps management monitor discount practices and understand how promotions affect sales performance and profitability.
