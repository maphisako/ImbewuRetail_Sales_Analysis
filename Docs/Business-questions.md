# Business Questions
This project addresses the following business questions for Imbewu Retail head of sales and COO.

## Management(head of sales and COO)
1. Revenue growth and decline in the 4 provinces the business operates in,
specifically targeting Western Cape where there is seem to be a problem.

2. Customer count growth and decline in Western Cape and across the country per loyalty tier.

3. Which category of products is most profitable?

4. Revenue trend by month for the 18 months period.

5. How the most expensive category of products performs against the least expensive category.

6. which stores are generating most revenue?
